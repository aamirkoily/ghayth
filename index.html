          08 · Built for the desert

          The desert rules

          💧

          Every litre has a job

          Water follows the real daily heat, not the calendar. Extra water is only given to wash salt out, and only when salt is near the limit, when each litre carries out the most salt.

          📶

          Works on one bar of signal

          A real SMS arrives every morning at 5:30, sent automatically: one plain-text message under 160 characters, no app, no data plan. The full 7-day plan arrives ahead, so a dead signal for a week changes nothing. This page installs and runs offline.

          🪙

          Costs about one EC meter

          No sensors in the ground. Free satellite-era weather data, a $20 salinity pen checked once a month, and any phone. Workers get it in Urdu, owners in Arabic.

          09 · How it works

          From weather to a decision

          1. WeatherDaily evaporation demand (ET₀), heat, rain. Live forecast + 2025 history.
          2. Palm needETc = ET₀ × Kc 0.9 × canopy 0.7 × spacing² (FAO-56).
          3. Soil-salt model8 layers of sandy soil. Water carries salt down; roots leave it behind.
          4. DecisionRefill what the palm used. Flush only when salt nears 4 dS/m (FAO-29 limit).
          5. MessageLitres per palm, farm total, flush day. Arabic, Urdu, English, voice.
          What is real, what is modelled
          • Real: 2025 daily weather for Al Ain and Liwa (Open-Meteo historical API, ERA5), and the live 7-day forecast.
          • Standard science: FAO-56 crop water need, FAO-29 date palm salt tolerance (no loss up to ECe 4 dS/m, then −3.6% per dS/m), FAO-29 leaching rule for comparison.
          • Modelled: the soil (sandy, 1.2 m root zone, 8 layers, field capacity 10%). Harvest is a relative index, not kg.
          • Your inputs: well salinity, soil salt reading, palm count, current routine. The farmer’s routine is whatever they enter; defaults are illustrative.
